THE MISSION

A window into
the extraordinary.

Cosmic Atlas is an independent interactive space observatory, built to make the Solar System tangible through 3D graphics and data-driven orbital motion.

REAL ASTRONOMY

Heliocentric planetary positions use JPL Horizons samples when available, otherwise NASA/JPL's published Keplerian elements. No arbitrary circular motion.

HONEST SCALE

Explore mode compresses distances and enlarges planet radii. Distance mode maps AU linearly, but planet sizes remain enlarged for visibility.

ILLUSTRATIVE TEXTURES

Planet maps are based on mission imagery with visual editing and gap filling. Clouds, glow, stars and lighting are artistic effects, not real-time spacecraft imagery.

FREE AND OPEN

Hosted on GitHub Pages, with a transparent data methodology and freely accessible source code.

Astronomical positions: NASA/JPL. Planet textures: Solar System Scope, CC BY 4.0. Space imagery and effects are visual approximations.

DISPLAY SETTINGS

Milky Way directions from ESA Gaia EDR3. Bright stars from the HYG catalog. Survey brightness is enhanced, not a naked-eye view.

Balanced viewing light improves surface visibility. Switch off for darker, more natural day/night contrast.

Linear mode scales orbit distances according to AU. Planet sizes are enlarged in both modes so they remain visible.
